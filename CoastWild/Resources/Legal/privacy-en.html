Coast & Wild Privacy Policy

Updated September 29, 2026

Information we handle

Anonymous authentication provides a device account identifier and credentials managed by our authentication provider. We keep a device identifier in protected credential storage to associate this device's records. The identifier alone cannot authenticate you. Trips, journals, saved content, learning progress, preferences and selected photos are stored locally for that account. We do not continuously collect location.

Network and web content

Authentication and private synchronization use our cloud service. Public learning content is downloaded and cached on this device. Legal and lesson web content is shown without a privileged app messaging channel.

Private note sync

After consent and sign-in, note titles, text, dates, tags, draft status and compressed photo backups (including photos in existing notes) are stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the US (us-east1). Each note links to its own images. Up to 12 JPEG backups per note, each at most 200 KB, are stored as Base64; Base64 is not encryption. Backups omit source photo metadata. Local originals remain unchanged. Anonymous authentication restricts access; authentication credentials are not stored in note documents. Same-device reinstall recovery is best-effort and requires the same retained anonymous identity, Keychain device identifier and account partition. UUID alone cannot grant access. Recovery is not guaranteed after reinstalling, clearing Keychain or changing devices.

Retention and your choices

Local content remains until you clear that space, delete your account, or remove the app. Returning to the welcome screen preserves your device credentials and records. Deleting a note also deletes its cloud photo backups when sync completes. Delete account first removes this device account's accessible cloud notes and photo backups, then its authentication account, then local content. Network access is required. If a step fails, deletion remains pending and can be retried; synchronization is paused during deletion. Data from a lost identity or an unavailable legacy partition may require support assistance. Removing the app alone does not delete cloud notes or photos.

Contact

Photo access occurs only for images you select. Contact support@coastwild.app with privacy questions.
