Coast & Wild Privacy Policy

Updated September 29, 2026

Information we handle

A remote account response supplies a user identifier and session token. We keep an installation-level device identifier in Keychain and UserDefaults for sign-in. Trips, journals, saved content, learning progress, preferences and selected photos are stored locally for that account. We do not continuously collect location.

Network and web content

Sign-in and configuration use the configured remote service. Public learning content is downloaded and cached on this device. Legal and lesson web content is shown without a privileged app messaging channel.

Private note sync

After consent and sign-in, note titles, text, dates, tags, draft status and compressed photo backups (including photos in existing notes) are stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the US (us-east1). Each note links to its own images. Up to 12 JPEG backups per note, each at most 200 KB, are stored as Base64; Base64 is not encryption. Backups omit source photo metadata. Local originals remain unchanged. Firebase anonymous authentication restricts access; business login tokens are not stored in Firestore. Same-device reinstall recovery is best-effort and requires the same retained anonymous identity, Keychain device identifier and account partition. UUID alone cannot grant access. Recovery is not guaranteed after reinstalling, clearing Keychain or changing devices.

Retention and your choices

Local content remains until you clear that space, delete your account, or remove the app. Logging out preserves it and stops note sync. Deleting a note also deletes its cloud photo backups when sync completes. Deleting the current account first deletes its accessible Firebase notes and photo backups, then local content and the session; it requires network access and failure is reported. This does not delete the separate business service account or inaccessible data from a lost anonymous identity. Removing the app alone does not delete cloud notes or photos.

Contact

Photo access occurs only for images you select. Contact support@coastwild.app with privacy questions.
