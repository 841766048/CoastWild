Coast & Wild Privacy Policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Information we handle

A remote account response supplies a user identifier and session token. We keep an installation-level device identifier in Keychain and UserDefaults for sign-in. Trips, journals, saved content, learning progress, preferences and selected photos are stored locally for that account. We do not continuously collect location.

Network and web content

Sign-in and configuration use the configured remote test service. Future remote web content will receive only documented app and session context. The business WebView is not enabled in this version.

Payments and attribution

In-app payment and attribution integrations are not enabled. If enabled later, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before data is collected.

Retention and your choices

Local content remains until you clear that space, delete your account, or remove the app. Logging out preserves it. The current delete-account network step is simulated: after success the app deletes local content and the session, but does not claim server-side deletion.

Contact

Photo access occurs only for images you select. Contact support@coastwild.app with privacy questions.
