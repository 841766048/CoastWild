Coast & Wild Privacy Policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Information we collect

We store the name and email you enter for your local account, together with trips, saved lessons, gear lists, journals and preferences you create. We do not continuously collect your location.

How information is used

This information supports sign-in, personal settings, trip planning and learning progress. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Local data and accounts

Account records and app content are stored on this device. Removing the app may remove local data, so export anything you want to keep first.

Photo access

Photo access is requested only when you select a trip cover or journal image. You can change access at any time in iOS Settings.

Your choices

You may export your content, clear the current account space, or stop using the app. Material policy changes will be shown before a new version is accepted.
