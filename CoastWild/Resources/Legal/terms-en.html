Account deletion

Account deletion removes this device account's accessible cloud notes and photo backups, deletes its authentication account, and then removes local records. Network access is required. If a step fails, deletion can be retried and synchronization remains paused. Unavailable data from a lost identity or legacy partition may require support assistance. Completed deletion cannot be undone.
