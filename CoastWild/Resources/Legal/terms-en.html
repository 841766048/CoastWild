Account deletion

The business-server deletion request remains simulated because no business account deletion API is configured. Deletion first removes this device's accessible Firebase notes and photo backups for the current account, then removes local content and the session. If cloud deletion fails, local content is retained for retry. This does not delete the account on the business server. Completed deletion cannot be undone.
